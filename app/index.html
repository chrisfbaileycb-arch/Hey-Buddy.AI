Hey Buddy Platform Guide & Safety

Overview of capabilities, safety guardrails, and recommended protocols.

⚡ What Hey Buddy Can Do

  • Custom Agent Fleet: Build specialized sub-agents with custom instructions and granted capabilities.
  • DAG Workflows: Connect multi-step automated workflows with variable interpolation (`{{step.output}}`).
  • Zero-Cost Free Models: Access OpenRouter Free Tier models (Llama 3.3 70B, DeepSeek R1, Gemini 2.0 Flash) for $0.
  • E2EE Cloud Sync: Sync your agent fleet securely across desktop and mobile devices using Web Crypto.

🚫 What Hey Buddy Cannot Do (Guardrails)

  • No Unauthorized Actions: Hey Buddy is a message-taker and workflow assistant—it never executes unauthorized real-world actions.
  • No Excessive Mobile Downloads: Device-guard protections block large 100B+ GGUF model downloads on mobile phone hardware to prevent crashes.

🔒 Safety & Privacy Protocols

  • Zero-Knowledge Encryption: All sensitive database records are encrypted client-side with AES-256-GCM.
  • Card Validation Protection: $0 card validation stops bot spammers and guarantees trial safety.
