Privacy Promise

Built different.
By design.

We built the privacy in first — not bolted on afterward. Your conversations are yours. Full stop.

✈️

Offline first

Every core feature works with airplane mode on. No network required to talk to your crew. We verify this in every build — PocketPal-style silent failures don't ship here.

🏠

Your device, your data

Conversations live in your device's storage, origin-scoped and encrypted. They don't touch our servers. There are no Hey Buddy servers holding your chats.

🔑

Keys stay local

If you connect your own AI API key, it's encrypted on your device using AES-256-GCM. It never leaves. It's never logged. We can't see it even if we wanted to.

☁️

API mode is visible

When a message routes to a cloud AI provider, you'll see a clear badge. No surprises. "Offline" means offline — we show you exactly what's happening, always.

🔗

E2EE device bridge

Connecting your phone to a home computer? Traffic is end-to-end encrypted before it leaves your device. Our relay — if used — sees only encrypted bytes. Never content.

💥

Nuclear delete

One button. Every conversation, every preference, every trace — gone from your device. No recovery path. Because you own your data, including the right to destroy it.
